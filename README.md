# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Positive behaviour includes:
- Showing respect and kindness.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Respecting different opinions.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and taking appropriate action when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including online platforms, project repositories, discussions, and related events.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behaviour may be reported to community maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

1. Warning: A private warning explaining the unacceptable behaviour.
2. Temporary action: A temporary restriction from community participation.
3. Permanent action: Permanent removal for serious or repeated violations.
